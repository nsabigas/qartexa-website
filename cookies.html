Cookie Preferences

How this website uses cookies, and how you can manage your preferences.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

At a glance
  • No advertising, analytics or other non-essential cookies are used.
  • That is why there is no cookie banner.
  • We will ask for your consent before adding any optional tracking.

1What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website can store on your device to remember settings or measure how the site is used. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels.

2Our current approach

At the time of the last update, the Qartexa website does not set advertising, analytics or other non-essential cookies, and it does not use tracking pixels. Your language choice (English or French) applies only to the page you are viewing and is not stored in a cookie.

Because there are no non-essential cookies, there is nothing for you to accept or switch off, and no cookie banner is shown.

3Your preferences

  • Advertising and marketing cookies: not used.
  • Analytics and performance cookies: not used.
  • Essential cookies: none are required for this website to work.

If we add analytics or other optional tools in the future, we will update this page and ask for your consent before they run, and you will be able to change your choice here at any time.

4Third-party websites and services

When you open a link to another site, such as Google Maps or a social network, that site may set its own cookies under its own policy. When you submit the Contact form, it is processed by FormSubmit, which may use technical cookies or similar technologies of its own. Please read their policies for details.

5Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Doing so may affect how third-party websites you visit behave, but it will not affect this website.

6Contact

Questions about cookies can be sent to info@qartexa.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
