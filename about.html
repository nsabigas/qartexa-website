About Qartexa

Local engineering. Global technology. Accountable delivery.

Established in Kigali in March 2019, Qartexa Ltd trades as Qartexa Technologies and serves Rwanda, East Africa and the wider African market.

Who we are

A Rwandan ICT systems integrator.

We design, implement and support secure, scalable technology environments. Our work connects infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud, enterprise software, physical security, power systems and managed services.

A permanent core team works with a verified network of certified specialists. Qartexa retains project management, warranty obligations and final technical sign-off.

Founded in Kigali in 2019, Qartexa was established to connect technology supply with reliable, locally available implementation and support. We remain engaged from design and integration through handover and ongoing service.

Our purpose

Technology that creates lasting value.

We combine global technologies, local expertise, partnerships and continuous innovation to support Africa’s digital economy.
